The data

  1. charted serves in the Match Charting Project
  2. Left out tiebreak points (), serves with no recorded direction () and no-ad deciding points
  3. serves analyzed, used for every league figure
  4. of them from the listed players, used for the player view and the per-player tests

Listed players have at least charted 1st serves on each side and at least charted matches. Every share and win rate has a confidence interval that allows for serves in the same match being alike. The formulas below are the ones the code runs.

What we can and cannot see

Recorded in the charts

  • Where each serve went: wide, body or T.
  • Who won the point, and whether the serve went in.
  • The score, so break points can be picked out.

Not recorded

  • Where the returner stood or which way they leaned.
  • What the returner expected.
  • The exact spot where the ball landed.

The charts only record where the serve went. Favorite-target share is the share of serves to the server's most-used target: if a returner always leaned toward that target, they would be right this often, and a server who split evenly across three targets would give . A server is called predictable on a side when the direction mix is lopsided and differs from the league average for the same opponents (mix test, q < ).

The idea being tested

The idea being tested

w_{\text{wide}} = w_{\text{body}} = w_{T}

If a server mixed optimally (minimax play, Walker and Wooders 2001), every direction would win the same share of points, so a returner would have nothing to exploit. A 1st serve's "win" counts the whole point, including points where the 1st serve missed.

Estimates

Formulas 1 to 5
1 Formula 1: Player share and win rateHow often a player serves each way, with a range.
\begin{aligned}&\hat p=\frac{\sum_g y_g}{N},\quad N=\sum_g m_g \\ &\widehat{SE}=\max\Big(\sqrt{\tfrac{G}{G-1}\sum_g\big(\tfrac{y_g-\hat p\,m_g}{N}\big)^2},\ \sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/N}\Big) \\ &\text{CI}=\big[\max(0,\hat p-t_{.975,G-1}\widehat{SE}),\ \min(1,\hat p+t_{.975,G-1}\widehat{SE})\big]\end{aligned}

A player's matches are pooled, and the standard error treats each match as a cluster but is never smaller than the plain binomial one. For a share, y counts serves to direction d and m counts serves in the situation; for a win rate, y counts points won after serving d and m counts serves to d.

Code: ratio, summarise in src/app_data.py

2 Formula 2: Favorite-target share and its percentileHow lopsided a player's aim is, compared with peers.
f=\max_d \hat s_d,\qquad \text{pct}_i=\frac{\#\{j\neq i: f_j<f_i\}}{n_{\text{group}}-1}

The favorite-target share f is the share of serves to the most-used direction on all points, and the percentile is the share of the other listed players in the same tour by serve by side group whose f is lower.

Code: summarise in src/app_data.py; percentile in load in src/export_site.py

3 Formula 3: League expectation for the same opponentsWhat the league does against the same kinds of opponents.
\bar s_d=\sum_k\pi_k\,s^{L}_{k,d},\qquad \bar w_d=\sum_k\pi_k\,w^{L}_{k,d}

League shares and win rates for each handedness matchup k are weighted by the player's own share of serves in that matchup; the league values pool break points and other points and include the player.

Code: summarise in src/app_data.py

4 Formula 4: Break-point shiftHow the aim changes when a break point is on the line.
\Delta_d=\frac{n^{BP}_d}{N^{BP}}-\frac{n^{\neg BP}_d}{N^{\neg BP}}

The share of serves to direction d on break points minus the share on all other points. For example, Rafael Nadal's deuce-side 1st serves went wide on of other points and of break points.

Code: summarise in src/app_data.py

5 Formula 5: Upper-bound gainThe most a server could gain from the best direction.
\text{gain}=100\,(\hat w_{d^*}-\hat w_{\text{all}}),\qquad d^*=\arg\max_{d:\,n_d\ge30}\hat w_d

Points per serves the server would gain by sending every serve to the direction that won most for them. It is shown only when the win test has q < , and it is an upper bound: it ignores that returners adapt and it picks the best of several noisy rates.

Code: summarise in src/app_data.py

Tests and corrections

Formulas 6 to 8
6 Formula 6: Mix compared with the leagueTests whether a player's mix differs from the league.
\begin{aligned}&u_g=\tfrac1N\begin{pmatrix}n_{g,\text{wide}}-m_g\hat s_{\text{wide}}\\ n_{g,T}-m_g\hat s_T\end{pmatrix},\quad \hat V=\tfrac{G}{G-1}\sum_g u_gu_g^\top,\quad \delta=\begin{pmatrix}\hat s_{\text{wide}}-\bar s_{\text{wide}}\\ \hat s_T-\bar s_T\end{pmatrix} \\ &W=\delta^\top\hat V^{-1}\delta,\qquad p=\Pr\big(F_{2,\,G-1}>W/2\big)\end{aligned}

A Wald test of whether the wide and T shares (body is the remainder) differ from the league expectation, with a match-clustered covariance; the league values are treated as fixed and there is no binomial floor.

Code: summarise in src/app_data.py

7 Formula 7: Generalized Cochran-Mantel-Haenszel testCompares serves within matches, so match differences cancel.
\begin{aligned}&D_i=\sum_h\Big(y_{hi}-n_{hi}\tfrac{S_h}{T_h}\Big) \\ &V_{ij}=\sum_h\frac{S_h(T_h-S_h)}{T_h-1}\Big(\frac{n_{hi}}{T_h}\delta_{ij}-\frac{n_{hi}n_{hj}}{T_h^2}\Big),\ i,j\le k-1 \\ &Q=D^\top V^{-1}D\ \sim\ \chi^2_{k-1}\end{aligned}

The strata h are matches with more than one serve, so differences between matches cancel. In the win test the groups are directions with at least serves and success is winning the point; in the break-point test the groups are break points and other points and success is serving to d. The p-value is the asymptotic chi-square one.

Code: cmh in src/app_data.py

8 Formula 8: Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rateKeeps false alarms in check across many tests.
q_{(i)}=\min_{j\ge i}\ \min\Big(1,\frac{m\,p_{(j)}}{j}\Big)

Each family is pooled across tours, serves and sides: m = mix tests, win tests and break-point tests. A flag means q < . There are flagged break-point tests on player by side by serve patterns.

Code: bh in src/app_data.py

League-wide estimates

Formulas 9 to 12
9 Formula 9: Two-way clustered varianceLeague error bars that allow for repeat servers and returners.
M_c=\frac{G_c}{G_c-1}\sum_{g\in c}\Big(\sum_{i\in g}u_i\Big)\Big(\sum_{i\in g}u_i\Big)^{\top},\qquad M=M_{\text{srv}}+M_{\text{ret}}-M_{\text{srv}\times\text{ret}}

League standard errors allow for correlation within the same server, the same returner and the same server-returner pair. If the combined matrix is not positive semidefinite, the one-way matrix with the larger trace is used; degrees of freedom are the smaller of the server and returner cluster counts minus one.

Code: twoway_meat in src/baseline.py

10 Formula 10: League direction intervalsRanges for league shares and win rates.
u_i=\frac{y_i-\hat p\,n_i}{N},\qquad \hat p\pm t_{.975,\min(G_s,G_r)-1}\sqrt{M_{11}}\ \text{ clipped to } [0,1]

League shares and win rates use the two-way clustered variance above, with no binomial floor.

Code: ratio_ci in src/baseline.py

11 Formula 11: Within-server comparisonsCompares each serve only with the same server's serves.
\begin{aligned}&\tilde y_i=y_i-\bar y_{f(i)},\quad \tilde x_i=x_i-\bar x_{f(i)},\quad \hat\beta=(\tilde X^\top\tilde X)^{-1}\tilde X^\top\tilde y \\ &u_i=\tilde x_i\,(\tilde y_i-\tilde x_i^\top\hat\beta),\qquad \hat V=\frac{N-1}{N-K}(\tilde X^\top\tilde X)^{-1}M(\tilde X^\top\tilde X)^{-1}\end{aligned}

Each serve is compared only with serves in the same fixed-effect group: server by side by matchup by break point for points won and serve in, and match by server by side for the break-point shift. The regressors are body and T indicators with wide as the baseline, or break-point and game-point indicators with the outcome "served to d"; K counts only these regressors.

Code: within_server in src/baseline.py

12 Formula 12: Serial repeatsChecks whether servers repeat the last direction.
R_h=\#\{t:d_{h,t}=d_{h,t-1}\},\qquad E_h=\frac{\sum_k n_{hk}(n_{hk}-1)}{n_h},\qquad \text{diff}=\frac{\sum_h(R_h-E_h)}{\sum_h(n_h-1)}

Within each match by server by side group (one tour and serve number, in point order, at least two serves), the number of serves that repeat the previous direction is compared with the number expected if the same serves came in random order. Consecutive 2nd serves are not consecutive points.

Code: serial_test in src/baseline.py

Replication

Formula 13
13 Formula 13: Split-half stabilityChecks whether patterns hold up in two halves of the matches.
f^{A}_i=\max_d\hat s^{A}_{i,d},\quad e^{A}_i=\hat w^{A}_{i,T}-\hat w^{A}_{i,\text{wide}},\qquad r=\operatorname{corr}_i(f^A_i,f^B_i)

Each player's matches are shuffled once (seed 0) and the first half goes to half A. The favorite-target share f and the T-minus-wide win edge e are computed in each half from plain proportions on 1st serves, and correlated across players within tour and side. This is one split, with no confidence interval and no Spearman-Brown correction.

Code: stability in src/app_data.py

Limits

  • Volunteers choose which matches to chart. Most are big matches of well-known players, not a random sample.

  • The data are observational. A server may pick a direction because of the returner's position or the score.

  • Returners adapt, so any gain figure is an upper bound.

  • A test that finds no difference does not show that there is none; a small edge may be too small to detect with this much data.

  • Tiebreak points, serves with no recorded direction and no-ad deciding points are left out.

  • Each rebuilt serve row was checked against the project's own serve-direction totals; the README reports the match rate.
